How it works:
Uses 4D simplex noise sampled on a torus for seamless wrapping. The smear effect applies motion blur along a direction while preserving tile edges.

Smear types:
• Directional - Blur along a fixed angle
• Radial - Blur outward from center
• Zoom - Blur toward/away from center
• Flow - Blur follows the noise pattern
